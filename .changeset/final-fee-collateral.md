Size collateral from the final fee. Collateral was sized once from the fee estimate before the final coin selection, collateral input and return, and final ex-units were known, so a completed script transaction could carry less than `collateralPercentage` of its fee (for example with `setMinFee`, expensive scripts, or a large reference-script fee) and be rejected by the ledger. The fee fixed point now tops the collateral up in place as the fee settles, shrinking the collateral return or adding collateral inputs, on every path (with and without coin selection, delayed redeemers, custom and provider evaluators, and wallet-supplied collateral). When adding inputs would exceed `maxCollateralInputs` (or the remaining UTxOs cannot cover the top-up), completion is replayed once with the collateral selected up front at the required amount, so a single larger UTxO can replace several small ones. Initial collateral selection respects the protocol's `maxCollateralInputs` instead of a fixed 3. Completion now fails with a clear error on insufficient collateral (for example when the wallet cannot cover the required collateral) instead of returning an invalid transaction. The emulator now rejects under-collateralised transactions as the ledger does.
