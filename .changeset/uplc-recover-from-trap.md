The Node.js builds recover from a wasm trap. A trap or a stack overflow inside wasm used to leave the instance unable to run anything after it, so every later call threw `RuntimeError: memory access out of bounds`. Now, after a call throws, the next call first replaces the instance with a fresh one; the error itself is rethrown unchanged. A result returned before the replacement throws when read afterwards, and freeing it, explicitly or by garbage collection, does not touch the new instance. This applies to the Node.js entries of `@lucid-evolution/uplc`, `@lucid-evolution/uplc/speed` and `@lucid-evolution/uplc/size`. The browser, worker, workerd and edge-light builds are unchanged and do not recover: a bundler instantiates their wasm once, so after a trap the page or worker has to load the package again. The bindings are now generated by wasm-bindgen 0.2.126.
