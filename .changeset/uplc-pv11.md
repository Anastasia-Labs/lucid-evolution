Support protocol version 11 (van Rossem) in the Aiken evaluator: the Plutus V3 array builtins and constants, the new Value and BLS builtins, `case` on built-in types, and the builtins that become available to Plutus V1 and V2. Evaluation follows the Haskell evaluator, plutus-core 1.71: builtins unlift their arguments before they are costed, integers and bytestrings passed to builtins are bounded as on chain, and costing saturates like Plutus, and scripts are scope-checked as the ledger does: before protocol version 12 a free variable inside `constr` or `case` is admitted and only fails the program if it is evaluated, whatever its de Bruijn index. `Data` is handled as Plutus handles it: before protocol version 11 `constrData` takes any integer as the constructor index, `serialiseData` gives the canonical encoding whatever encoding the `Data` was decoded from, and `equalsData` compares negative big integers by value. Transactions are evaluated at protocol version 11 only when `eval_phase_two_raw` is given that version; omitting it keeps protocol version 10. Scripts are decoded as the ledger decodes them: a program's UPLC version must be available to its Plutus version at the protocol version, a constructor has at most 1024 fields, a constant's type is bounded in size, nothing may follow a flat program, and nothing may follow a Plutus V3 script's CBOR. As the ledger does, `eval_phase_two_raw` fails a Plutus V3 script that returns anything other than unit, and a builtin whose parameters are missing from the given cost models costs the maximum, so no budget affords it. `verifyEd25519Signature` returns false for public keys and signature points of small order and for public keys not encoded canonically, as libsodium does. Scripts nesting terms thousands of levels deep are decoded and evaluated without overflowing the stack.

Add `eval_program_raw`, which evaluates a flat-encoded UPLC program applied to CBOR `Data` arguments outside of any transaction, at any protocol version from 5 to 12 (Dijkstra, where UPLC 1.2.0 programs can `case` on `Data`), and returns whether it succeeded, why not, its cost and its traces, and `uplc_build_info`, which describes the build: the uplc crate it was built from, each Plutus version's cost model length, and a self-test of its semantics.
